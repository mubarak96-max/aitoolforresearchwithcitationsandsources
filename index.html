Why this site exists

Fluent is not the same as true

AI writes a confident paragraph about anything. In research, that is the risk: a wrong claim reads like a right one, and an invented reference looks like a real one. A 2023 study in Scientific Reports found that most references produced by an earlier ChatGPT model were fabricated, and that later models still invented some.

So our guides judge tools on one thing first: how quickly you can check them. Then we show you the method behind each stage of research, so the tool supports your judgement instead of replacing it.

Common questions

Can AI do research for me?

AI can search the literature, summarise papers and draft text much faster than you can, but it cannot take responsibility for the result. Use it to find and read sources, then check every citation and write the argument yourself.

What is the best AI tool for academic research?

The best tool is the one whose claims you can verify. Look for an assistant that searches real scholarly indexes, links every claim to a paper you can open, answers from your own PDFs with page numbers, and exports into your citation style. Our nine-test checklist shows how to compare tools.

Do AI tools make up citations?

General chatbots can, because they generate references from memory. Research tools that retrieve records from databases such as OpenAlex, Semantic Scholar or PubMed are much less likely to, but a real citation can still be used to support a claim the paper does not make, so always read the source.
